IA Rebalancing

IA Rebalancing is a large-scale balance component for Improved Anvil that refines encounters, spells, abilities, items and kits to increase build diversity and make more tactical options viable, while (hopefully) preserving the original spirit and difficulty of IA.

For those that play with Sibel Mod installed, but consider the items created by Lady Elowen to be too powerful, I also introduced a small component that attempts to rebalance the items she can create, either by toning them down, or by heavily increasing their price. Items belonging to Sibel remain untouched.

Since IA Rebalancing looks through all files and patches them when needed, mods that change/introduce new items/spells/creatures should be installed before IA Rebalancing.

This component works only with BG2EE version 2.6.6
