New and rebalanced kits/classes

Almost all kits and classes are at least somewhat affected by IA Rebalancing and Controversial Changes components, so please read all descriptions. Here I present a list of those kits/classes that were heavily reworked

1. Paladin class

PALADIN: A Paladin is a warrior bold and pure, the exemplar of everything good and true. Like the Fighter, the Paladin is a person of action and combat. However, the Paladin lives for the ideals of righteousness, justice, honesty, piety, and chivalry and strives to be a living example of these virtues so that others may learn from  as well as gain by  actions.

CLASS FEATURES:

- May wear helmets.
- May wear any armor and use any weapon.
- May not exceed Specialization (two slots) in any weapon class.
- May allocate three slots in Sword and Shield Style, Single Weapon Style and Two Handed Weapon Style.
- May allocate two slots in Two-Weapon Style.
- May use Lay On Hands ability once per day to heal a target for 5 Hit Points per level of the Paladin. Starts with one use and gains another one at level 15 and 30.
- May cast Detect Evil once per day per level (starts at 1st level with 4 uses).
- May cast Protection From Evil 10' Radius once at level 5, gains additional uses at levels 15, 25 and 35.
- May Turn Undead as a Cleric two levels lower, starting at level 3.
- May cast priest spells starting at level 9.
- Receives a +2 bonus to all Saving Throws.
- Receives a +2 bonus to critical hit with all swords apart from short swords.
- Has a passive ability called Aura of Courage, which removes effects of fear from all nearby allies every 6 seconds, if the Paladin is not incapacitated in any way (stunned, feared, charmed, confused etc.)
- At level 12 gains one use of Heavenly Shield clerical spell and can use it as an innate ability.
- At level 14 gains passive ability called Plate Armor Mastery.

PLATE ARMOR MASTERY

Mastery of the heaviest armors grants the Paladin +1 AC and +5% physical resistance while wearing plate or full plate.

- Alignment restricted to lawful good.
- Hit Die: d10

2. Cavalier

CAVALIER: This class represents the most common picture of the knight: the gentleman warrior who epitomizes honor, courage and loyalty. He is specialized in battling 'classical' evil monsters such as demons and dragons.

Advantages:
-  All melee hits against demons, devils and dragons have 10% chance to prevent regeneration of those creatures for 3 seconds, items, spells and abilities that instantly heal may still be used
-  At levels 10, 20, 30 and 40 gains one use of Guardian's Stand ability
-  At levels 12, 20, 28 and 36 gains one use of Cleansing Light ability
-  Immune to charm
-  Immune to poison
-  20% resistance to fire
-  20% resistance to acid

GUARDIAN'S STAND

By planting their feet and raising their shield, the Cavalier creates a protective barrier against incoming fire and missiles. Through steady defense and trained reflexes, they deflect much of the harm aimed at those nearby.

While maintaining this stance, the Cavalier is immobile and unable to take other actions. All allies within 4 feet gain 50% resistance to fire and missile damage for up to 6 seconds, or until the Cavalier ends the stance early by using Finish Guardian's Stand.

Both Guardian's Stand and Finish Guardian's Stand can be used instantly, even if the Cavalier's aura is not cleansed.

CLEANSING LIGHT

The Cavalier calls upon divine strength to purge all effects that weaken resolve or cloud judgment, removing all effects of Doom, Greater Doom, Greater Malison and Aura of Malison and Field of Dark Magic from himself or one touched creature.

Disadvantages:
-  May not use missile weapons
-  Does not gain access to Plate Armor Mastery passive ability at level 14
-  May allocate only one slot in Two-Handed Weapon Style and Two-Weapon Style
-  May not use 'lay on hands' ability more than once per day

3. Thief class

THIEF: To accomplish  goals, for good or ill, the Thief is a skilled pilferer. Cunning, nimbleness, and stealth are  hallmarks. Whether  turns  talent against innocent passersby and wealthy merchants or oppressors and monsters is a choice for the Thief to make.

CLASS FEATURES:

- May not wear armor heavier than studded leather.
- May not equip shields larger than bucklers.
- May only use the following weapons: long sword, short sword, katana, scimitar, dagger, club, quarterstaff, crossbow, shortbow, dart, sling.
- May only become Proficient (one slot) in any weapon class.
- May only become Proficient (one slot) in any fighting style.
- May distribute 10 points per level (40 at level 1) in thieving abilities: Open Locks, Pick Pockets, Find Traps, Stealth Strike, Hide In Shadows, Detect Illusion, Set Traps.
- May put up to 100 points into any Thieving Ability and up to 150 into Pick Pockets.
- Starts with base scores of 40% in Open Locks, Find Traps and Pick Pockets.
- May backstab for 2x damage.
- May use the Set Snare ability once per day. Gains one use at level 1 and an additional use every 5 levels thereafter.

SET SNARE: Set a trap in the chosen location when no hostile creatures are in sight. Traps grow more powerful with the Thief's level and can only be triggered by enemies.
  1st level: Deals 2d8+5 missile damage.
  11th level: Deals 2d8+5 missile damage and additionally deals 2d6 poison damage per round for the next 3 rounds.
  16th level: Deals 3d8+5 missile damage and 4d8+2 fire damage.
  21st level: Deals 3d8+5 missile damage and 20 poison damage with no save; slays target if a Save vs. Death with a +4 bonus is failed.

- Alignment restricted to any except lawful good.
- Hit Die: d6

Prime Requisites For Dual-Classing: Dexterity

4. Assassin

ASSASSIN: This is a killer trained in discreet and efficient murder, relying on anonymity and surprise to perform his task.

Advantages:
- May coat his weapon in poison once per day per 4 levels. The next hit with that weapon will inject the poison into the target, dealing out 2 damage per second for 12 seconds. A saving throw vs. poison limits damage to 12 total. The weapon will remain poisoned for 3 rounds.
- Bonus +1 to hit and +1 damage.
- Starts with base score of 40% in Stealth Strike and Hide in Shadows.
- May put up to 200 points into Stealth Strike and Hide in Shadows.
- May become Specialized (two slots) in Short Sword and Dagger.
- May become Master (three slots) in Single Weapon Style.

Disadvantages:
- Only 10 points to distribute at level 1.
- May put only 100 points into Pick Pockets, 75 points into Find Traps and only 50 points into Detect Illusions.
- Does not get standard Thief bonuses to Open Locks, Find Traps and Pick Pockets.

5. Beast Master

BEAST MASTER: This Ranger is a wanderer and is not comfortable in civilized lands. Therefore,  maintains a natural affinity for animals; they are  friends and comrades-in-arms, and the Beast Master has a limited form of telepathic communication with them.

Advantages:
- Can reach Mastery (three slots) in all weapons that he can use.
- May achieve Mastery (three slots) in Two-Handed Weapon Style.
- Can use Hunter's Mark ability unlimited number of times.
- Gains access to two additional spells, a 2nd level spell Animal Fortitude and a 3rd level spell Animal Rage.
- Can cast a special version of Animal Summoning I, which lasts for 15 rounds and can be cast unlimited number of times, but only once per 15 rounds.
- At levels 10, 12 and 14 gains analogous versions of Animal Summoning II, III and IV respectively.
- Can pick Animal Summoning V as a High Level Ability.
- At level 10, gains one use of the Unified Minds ability. Gains an additional use of this skill for every 10 levels thereafter.

HUNTER'S MARK:
When using this ability, the Beast Master places a mystical mark on a single enemy. For the next 15 rounds the marked enemy suffers a -2 penalty to their THAC0 against the Beast Master and any animals. The Beast Master and all animals within a 15-foot radius gain a +2 bonus to their damage and THAC0 against the marked enemy. Additionally, all attacks from affected animals against that target will be treated as if made with a +4 weapon for determining if the target can be damaged. All thac0 and damage bonuses/penalties increase by 1 for every 5 levels of the caster.

The Beast Master can use this ability again after 15 rounds or if the target dies.

UNIFIED MINDS:
By invoking this ability, the Beast Master forms a mental bond with one animal companion, linking their senses and instincts into a single, harmonious flow. For 1 turn, as long as the Beast Master and the chosen animal remain within 10 feet of each other, they instinctively protect one another - deflecting blows, alerting each other to danger, and shifting to shield vital areas.

While under this effect, both the Beast Master and the bonded creature receive a -5 bonus to Armor Class and 10% damage resistance against all weapon types. Their shared awareness also shields them from mental interference, granting immunity to fear and protection from confusion, charm, domination, and similar effects, akin to the Chaotic Commands spell.

This ability has no effect if either the Beast Master or the animal is confused, stunned, charmed, feared, or feebleminded at the time of casting or restoring the link. The link is broken if they move more than 10 feet apart, but it will be restored if they come close again before the duration ends.

The effects do not stack with multiple uses.

Disadvantages:
- Spellcasting limited to 3rd level Divine spells
- May only use simple non-bladed weapons.
- May not wear armor heavier than studded leather.
- Cannot dual class.

6. Abjurer

ABJURER: A Mage who specializes in protective magics.

Advantages:
- May cast one additional spell per level.
- Receives a +15% bonus when scribing scrolls from the Abjuration school.
- Receives a +2 bonus when making Saving Throws against spells from the Abjuration school.
- Targets suffer a -2 penalty when making Saving Throws against spells from the Abjuration school.
- Casts all arcane spells as if 1 level higher.
- At levels 12, 18 and 24, 30 and 36 Abjurers get Abjuration Mastery ability.

ABJURATION MASTERY: With this power a skilled Abjurers can cast any spell of their favored school bypassing the need to cleanse their aura. The chosen spell is uninterruptible and does not need to be prepared in advance. This grants Abjurers an unparalleled edge in battle, allowing them to summon protective and dispelling magic at a moment's notice.

The potency of this ability grows with the caster's experience:

At level 12: Cast spells up to 4th level
At level 14: Cast spells up to 5th level
At level 16: Cast spells up to 6th level
At level 18: Cast spells up to 7th level
At level 20: Cast spells up to 8th level
At level 22 and above: Cast any mage abjuration spell

Disadvantages:
- May not learn or cast any spells of the Alteration school.
- Receives a -15% penalty when scribing scrolls from other schools.
- Cannot dual class.

Requires at least 15 Wisdom.

7. Conjurer

CONJURER: A Mage who specializes in creating creatures and objects to assist .

Advantages:

8. Diviner

DIVINER: A Mage who specializes in detection and divining magics.

Advantages:

GLIMPSE OF THE FUTURE: This ability targets any single friendly creature without requiring the caster's aura to be cleansed. It sends visions of numerous potential futures to the target, providing insight on how to avoid or mitigate impending danger. The target gains a +20 bonus to all saving throws and armor class for 2 seconds.

Disadvantages:

- Receives a -15% penalty when scribing scrolls from other schools.
- Cannot dual class.

Requires at least 16 Wisdom.

9. Enchanter

ENCHANTER: A Mage who specializes in manipulating the minds of sentient beings.

Advantages:
- May cast one additional spell per level.
- Receives a +15% bonus when scribing scrolls from the Enchantment school.
- Receives a +2 bonus when making Saving Throws against spells from the Enchantment school.
- Targets suffer a -2 penalty when making Saving Throws against spells from the Enchantment school.
- Gains an additional +1 saving throw against spells from Enchantment school every 6 levels.
- Gains one use of Focused Mind Blast ability at level 10, gains additional uses at levels 20 and 30.

FOCUSED MIND BLAST: Expertise with mind-affecting spells and creativity can make for a deadly combination. Enchanters can usurp control over enemy minds by force, or guide and join the minds of those who willingly allow them access. By casting this spell, an enchanter connects with companions from the same party and momentarily focuses on a small area within their vision. Through the enchanter's guidance, a powerful blast of cumulative telepathic energy is sent towards that point, exploding suddenly. Any creature in that area will be severely damaged, regardless of whether it has a mind or not.

The caster can achieve a similar effect alone, but the more party members join forces, the more deadly the attack will be. At level 10, the blast deals 4d6 magical damage plus an additional 2d6 for each party member. This damage increases by 2d6 for the caster and by 1d6 for party members at caster levels 16, 22, 28, 34 and 40, and it bypasses magical resistance. A party member has to be within 50 feet from the center of the spell to contribute in the damage.

- Enchanters will automatically learn a unique 4th level spell Emotion: Hope at level 7.

Disadvantages:
- May not learn or cast any spells of the Invocation school.
- Receives a -15% penalty when scribing scrolls from other schools.
- Cannot dual class.

Requires at least 16 Charisma.

10. Invoker

INVOKER: A Mage who specializes in the manipulation of raw and elemental energies.

Advantages:
- May cast one additional spell per level.
- Receives a +15% bonus when scribing scrolls from the Invocation school.
- Receives a +2 bonus when making Saving Throws against spells from the Invocation school.
- Targets suffer a -2 penalty when making Saving Throws against spells from the Invocation school.
- Invokers learn to manipulate elemental energies better than any other arcane casters. At level 10 all elemental damage they deal will be incremented by 5%, this bonus will grow to 10% at level 20 and to 15% at level 30.
- At level 15 gains one use of Chain Sequencer, gains another use at level 25, though as with any other Sequencer, only one instance of the spell can be active at the same time.

CHAIN SEQUENCER: This is a far more dangerous form of standard Sequencer spell. The caster first chooses one spell of levels 1-3 which will be put into the sequencer and stored as an ability just like with any other Sequencer. When used, that selected spell will be cast on all non-invisible hostile creatures in a 15-ft radius from the center chosen by the caster. It is worth mentioning, that spells which require touch will have no effects.

Disadvantages:
- May not learn or cast any spells of the Enchantment school.
- Receives a -15% penalty when scribing scrolls from other schools.
- Cannot dual class.

Requires at least 16 Constitution.

11. Transmuter

TRANSMUTER: A Mage who specializes in magic that alters physical reality.

Advantages:
- May cast one additional spell per level.
- Receives a +15% bonus when scribing scrolls from the Alteration school.
- Receives a +2 bonus when making Saving Throws against spells from the Alteration school.
- Targets suffer a -2 penalty when making Saving Throws against spells from the Alteration school.
- Transmuter gains additional attacks per round just like warrior classes, when they wield a weapon that they're proficient in (1/2 attack for specialization and 1 attack per round for grandmastery).
- Transmuter gains a special ability at levels 10/20/30/40, which acts just like Stoneskin, but can be cast on another character, however this version of the spell lasts for 3 turns only.
- Tenser's Transformation spell cast by Transmuters grants two additional slots in all weapons available to Mages, two slots in Single Weapon Style and in Two-handed Weapon Style and 1 additional base attack per round. Special Abilities button is also available when under the spell's effect.
- Tenser's Partial Transformation spell cast by Transmuters grants one point in Single Weapon Style and Two-handed Weapon Style and one additional attack per round.
- At level 14, 21, 28 and 35 gains one use of Blinding Speed ability.

BLINDING SPEED: This special spell makes the caster so fast, that he can traverse long distances in a blink of an eye, if there are no obstacles in his path. In addition, the number of attacks per round is doubled and this spell can't be dispelled. It lasts for 5 rounds.

- At level 18 and 24 gains one use of Dragon's Strength ability.

DRAGON'S STRENGTH: This spell endows the target with the raw power and vitality of a mighty dragon. This surge of strength allows the recipient to perform incredible feats of physical might and endure far greater punishment in combat. However, despite these formidable enhancements, their outward appearance remains unchanged, making the transformation all the more astonishing.

This spell grants the recipient 25 strength, +5 base damage in melee, immunity to Fear and a 25% increase in their base health pool, which can not be dispelled for its duration.

Disadvantages:
- May not learn or cast any spells of the Abjuration school.
- Receives a -15% penalty when scribing scrolls from other schools.
- Cannot dual class.

Requires at least 15 Dexterity.

12. Priestess of Shar (available only to Viconia)

PRIESTESS OF SHAR: Shar is the goddess of darkness, loss, and secrets, revered by those who dwell in shadow and silence. She embodies night, forgetfulness, and hidden knowledge, offering comfort to the grieving and power to those who seek to hide or destroy the light. Shar's faithful uphold her ideals through quiet manipulation, forbidden magic, and the eradication of joy and memory. As mistress of the night and twin to Selûne, she represents the ever-present allure of oblivion and the silence that follows sorrow.

Advantages:
- From level 20, Armor of Faith cast by this kit will grant 15% resistances, last for 50% longer and will be undispellable.
- Magic Resistance divine spell cast by Priestess of Shar can be directed at other creatures.
- Magic Damage dealt by Priestess of Shar is increased by 25%.
- Additional first level spell: Magic Missile 
- Additional second level spell: Mirror Image 
- Additional fourth level spell: Improved Invisibility 
- Additional fifth level spells: Immunity: Divination, Protective Chant
- Additional sixth level spell: Life Link
- Additional seventh level spells: Spare the Dying, Prismatic Spray
- Gains early access to a High Level Ability Purify Aura, may cast it once at level 10.
- From level 15 can use Aegis of Faith ability.

AEGIS OF FAITH: Through prayer and devotion, the cleric grants themselves a protective blessing that safeguards their magical defenses. For the next turn, any effects that targets and removes specific protections will be nullified. This spell does not protect against spells and effects that dispel all types of effects at once, like Remove Magic or Purge Magic.

Disadvantages:
- Alignment restricted to evil or true neutral.
- Can't use Sunray, Sol's Searing Orb, and False Dawn.

- May cast a special version of Hold Undead once per day. Gains one use at level 1 and an additional use every 5 levels thereafter.

- Additional fifth level spell: Protective Chant.
- Additional sixth level spell: Life Link.
- Additional seventh level spell: Spare the Dying.
- May cast Boon of Lathander once per day. Gains one use at level 10 and another use every 6 levels thereafter.

BOON OF LATHANDER: This spell lasts for 1 turn. It gives the caster a +1 bonus to all Saving Throws, +2 to damage and thac0, and +1 extra attack per round.

AEGIS OF FAITH: Through prayer and devotion, the cleric grants themselves a protective blessing that safeguards their magical defenses. SEEKING SWORD: This spell creates a long sword in the Cleric's inventory that only Priests of Helm can use, it stays in  equipment for 1 round per level, up to 20 rounds. The sword can only be used in main hand and to wield the weapon better, the caster must know how to use Long Swords. The sword gets more powerful as the caster gains experience. 20-24: 2-4 + 4 dmg, +4 thaco, 2 APR, priest caster level -4, range increased to 2 feet, +5% chance for critical hit
25-40: 2-6 + 5 dmg, +5 thaco, 2 APR, priest caster level -5, range increased to 2 feet, +10% chance for critical hit.

EXALTATION: This spell enables a priest to aid and protect any one being other than themselves. By touch, the caster removes the effects of fear, sleep, feeblemind, unconsciousness, and intoxication, as well as berserk and confused states of mind. In addition, the recipient is protected against spells and other attacks that cause these effects for 1 turn.

- May cast Divine Favor once per day every 10 levels of the caster (starts at 1st level with one use).

DIVINE FAVOR: This spell grants the caster a +1 bonus to hit and damage rolls every 3 levels of the caster, capped at +6. The spell lasts for 6 rounds.

17. Priest of Tempus

PRIEST OF TEMPUS: Tempus, also known as the Lord of Battles or the Foehammer, is random in his favors, yet his chaotic nature favors all sides equally. Lord Tempus may be on an army's side one day and against them the next; such is the nature of war. Tempuran clergy can be found on both sides of a conflict, as none can ever truly know whom the war god will favor.

Advantages:
- From level 20, Armor of Faith cast by this kit will grant 15% resistances, last for 50% longer and will be undispellable.
- Holy Power cast by Priest of Tempus lasts for 50% longer, gives additional 1/2 APR at level 12 and 1 APR from level 20, additionally it becomes undispellable.
- Additional fifth level spell: Protective Chant.
- Additional sixth level spell: Life Link.
- Additional seventh level spell: Spare the Dying.
- May cast Chaos of Battle once per day. Gains one use at level 1 and an additional use every 10 levels thereafter.
- Champion's Might's periodic pulses can affect up to two nearest characters at the same time.

CHAOS OF BATTLE:
This spell ensures that the next strike from all affected allies will be a critical hit, while the next strike from all affected enemies will be a critical miss. The spell lasts for 1 round. If a creature does not attack during this period, the spell's effect will be wasted on that target.

The area of effect of this spell grows with the caster's level as follows:
At levels 5-10, it affects all creatures within a 5-foot radius.
At levels 11-15, the area is increased to a 10-foot radius.
From level 16 onward, the area is increased to a 15-foot radius.

- From level 15 can use Aegis of Faith ability.

AEGIS OF FAITH: Through prayer and devotion, the cleric grants themselves a protective blessing that safeguards their magical defenses. For the next turn, any effects that targets and removes specific protections will be nullified. This spell does not protect against spells and effects that dispel all types of effects at once, like Remove Magic or Purge Magic.

18. Stalker

STALKER: Stalkers serve as covert intelligence-gatherers, comfortable in both wilderness and urban settings. They are the spies, informants, and interrogators, and their mastery of stealth makes them deadly opponents.

Advantages:
-  Can backstab with 2x multiplier.
-  Gains points in both Hide in Shadows and Stealth Strike on level up.
-  May achieve Mastery (three slots) in Single Weapon Style.
-  +20% to Hide in Shadows and Stealth Strike abilities.
-  12th level: May memorize 2 Mage spells: Haste, Protection from Normal Missiles.
-  Has access to Marked for the Kill ability.

MARKED FOR THE KILL: With a keen hunter's eye, the Stalker identifies and exploits the subtle flaws in an enemy's defenses. The target loses any immunity to backstab until any damage is dealt to him or until one round passes.

This ability does not take up the Stalker's action and can be used an indefinite number of times, however no more often than once per 4 rounds. This time is decreased to 3 rounds at level 10, 2 rounds at level 20 and finally to 1 round at level 30.

Disadvantages:
-  May not wear armor heavier than studded leather.
